# BeCartoonTV — Brand overview

**Original 2D characters. Songs, stories and playful discovery.**

BeCartoonTV is developing an original cartoon world for children and families, featuring Ben, Maya, Tiko, Zip and Rio. The brand's creative direction combines bright illustrated settings, musical participation, kind humour and small adventures built around curiosity and friendship.

## Meet the cast
- **Ben:** curious explorer; black hair, blue sheep shirt, blue shorts, red sneakers.
- **Maya:** creative spark; two hair puffs with turquoise ties, coral dress, turquoise shorts, yellow sneakers.
- **Tiko:** gentle yellow elephant with a turquoise bandana.
- **Zip:** energetic blue rabbit with a lime-green bowtie.
- **Rio:** warm golden lion with a red open vest and purple star badge.

## Collaboration conversations
Family entertainment, original stories and songs, creative educational projects, character-led experiences and other relevant brand collaborations. Each project requires a separate agreement on scope, permissions and rights.
