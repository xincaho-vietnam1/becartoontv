# BeCartoon World

## Our original story world
Beyond a rainbow bridge is a small island with a golden path. It connects Music Meadow, Curiosity Clubhouse and Rainbow Lagoon. Every little question can lead to a new adventure — and every friend has something different to offer.

Ben asks the first question. Maya imagines a way forward. Tiko helps the gang try again. Zip brings a joyful burst of energy. Rio makes sure everyone gets their moment to shine.

These are fictional character personalities and settings developed for BeCartoonTV. The attached original turnaround sheets remain the authority for every character's appearance.

## The journey
1. **Music Meadow:** meet, move and find a rhythm together.
2. **Curiosity Clubhouse:** turn an everyday problem into a thoughtful game or story.
3. **Rainbow Lagoon:** take the idea into a new setting, help a friend and return with a discovery.

The locations are neighbours on one island. Characters travel along the path and bridge; scenes do not randomly teleport between unrelated worlds.

## Story seeds
- **The Missing Beat:** everyone brings one sound. Together they turn an unfinished tune into a sing-along.
- **The Map With No Arrows:** each friend notices a different clue; cooperation leads them to a picnic spot.
- **The Little Paper Boat:** a boat gets stuck near the bridge; patient teamwork brings it safely back.
- **Zip's Quiet Moment:** a lively game becomes a listening adventure when the gang hears a tiny bird.
- **Rio Shares the Stage:** a solo performance becomes a joyful group song when every friend gets a turn.

## Creative promise
Warm original 2D stories, purposeful music, clear actions, stable character identities and positive themes. Every episode needs its own idea, beginning, development and resolution. Musical repetition should invite participation, not replace storytelling.

## Visual continuity
Keep the five supplied character designs unchanged. Use flat illustrated environments, dark outlines, sunshine yellow, turquoise, coral and green. Keep motion expressive and readable. Check anatomy, props, clothing, timing and audio before publication.
